**Blocking contract corrections**

1. **PartitionOrder cannot absorb the raw lowered KeyOrder.** `ordered_positions` keeps clustered input order and otherwise sorts by hash, then typed values, then row (`domain/operator/contract/partition.rs:36-68`). The lowered path sorts by raw u64 bit words then row with no clustered passthrough, and `hits` binary-searches on that bit order (`domain/positions/service.rs:149-176`); `firsts` yields `Keeps` marks, not an order (`178-198`). Callers are `domain/fragment/service/join.rs:143,151`, `domain/fragment/service.rs:339`, `domain/joins/service.rs:104` (antijoin/union), `domain/selections/service.rs:124` (project). One kind with two ordering contracts is not one semantic contract. This forces the 13th-kind decision, or an explicit statement that lowered KeyOrder stays outside the family. Separately, these are domain modules that cannot name the application owner, so "same owner scoped acquisition" needs an owner-scoped builder handle passed through `LoweredRoom`/`Work`.

2. **Port shape lacks recycle and take-out routes.** Payload buffers are reused in place: `key.rs:37-40`, `graph.rs:145-148`, `indexes.rs:328-342`, and the cursor at `closure_partitions.rs:174-184`. The engine also constructs a payload directly (`closure_partitions.rs:70-78`) and moves the raw `ClosurePartition` out with its lease (`187-195`) into `PrimitiveLease.closure` (`execution/lease.rs:185-189`, `invocation/observation.rs:19`). With `build -> DerivedPayload` and private constructors this has no route. Minimal fix: `build(prepared, inputs, &mut BuildWorkspace, recycled: Option<DerivedPayload>)`, scoped builds holding `DerivedPayload` by value, and a footprint descriptor replacing `AcquiredView::partition_order_capacity` (`view.rs:41-51`).

3. **Shared sub-allocation aliases are unexpressible under opaque payload plus borrowed-only views.** The engine clones inner `Arc<Vec<u32>>` out of payloads: `selection.rs:243-247` into `StoredSelection.rows` (`domain/table_model/types.rs:113`), `fanout.rs:156-169`, `graph/identity.rs:94-95` into `Population.positions`, `graph_key.rs:6-8` as `Weak<Vec<u32>>`, `graph/build.rs:156-165` into `GraphDomain.members`, and `graph/build.rs:134-138` borrows another payload's vertices. Ledger keys are inner-Arc addresses with capacity units (`backings.rs:4-10,26,38,44-49`, `graph.rs:246-260`). The design must either add a portable shareable positions handle with a stable allocation key and capacity, or alias whole payload Arcs and change `StoredSelection.rows`, `LiteralPostings.rows`, `Population.positions`, `RootAnswer.rows`, `GraphDomain.members`. "Live aliases never copy Vec payloads" with private fields is not satisfiable as written.

4. **Replacing `Entry.index` changes reclamation.** Today `EqualityShared(Arc<KeyIndex>)` wraps the backend Arc, so alias counting works by strong counts (`owner/graph_policy.rs:52,78-83,97-112`; `owner.rs:129-134`) and backend bytes are charged separately (`acquisition.rs:250-256,762`). With one `Arc<DerivedPayload>` in both `cache.entries` and `State.entries[].retained`, strong count == 1 never holds and index reclamation under graph pressure silently stops. State the alias rule per payload key and where the backend byte charge lives.

5. **NativeLookup has an unlisted direct fallback and a decline-as-fault mapping.** `optimized/ready.rs:15-47` calls `tree_finding` and `join_tree` outside the family; `invocation.rs:167` gates on it too. The port maps `None` to `WrongOperation` (`native.rs:41-42`). Decide decline versus fault, list `optimized/ready.rs` as a route, and fix `DerivedView::NativeLookup(Finding)` which is by value, not `&'a`.

6. **Identity corrections.** Pointer identity sites: `acquisition.rs:136`, `selection.rs:185` (Distinct context), `preparation.rs:38,111` and `owner/selection.rs:72` (selector map), `fanout.rs:27-33` (`source_bits` address). Distinct Arcs for one Equality request exist in production: RequestViews interning (`derived/request.rs:331-350`, one per request at `file_dag/request.rs:51`), column compile (`optimized/column.rs:41-44`), selection equalities (`preparation.rs:99-106`); only the stored Equality path de-duplicates them (`indexes.rs:213-218`). The canonical identity must bind, for Distinct, the predicate identity it consumes; for Membership, `membership_order` (`invocation.rs:285`); for graph keys, the live-position content compare (`graph_key.rs:34-46`); and replace fanout address identity with population identity. Counted multi-consumer proof is missing for 11 kinds and for the "both optional budgets 0 while required live" case.

7. **Second retention owner survives.** `cache.fanouts` retains postings with no ledger lease (`fanout.rs:163-172`); `graph_reclaim_credit` cannot reclaim it. State that fanout moves under `LeaseClass::Optional`. The "literal-OR physical postings consumers" route does not exist at this commit; the only reader is fanout reuse (`fanout.rs:28-39`).

8. **Callbacks and faults are not portable yet.** `DerivedInputs::Predicate.program: &mut dyn PredicateProgram` and `Dfs.symbols: &mut QuerySymbols` (`types.rs:122,137`) are engine-owned mutable callbacks (`selection/predicate.rs:10-42`). `DerivedFault` must preserve `MissingInput{position}`, `NoSuchColumn`, `DuplicateOrder` (`ordered.rs:55-57`), `ShapeMismatch`, `NotRun`, `WrongOperation`, and an opaque sticky engine error, and be `PartialEq` because `intern_missing` compares stored errors (`derived/request.rs:315`).

9. **Hardcoded 12 sites are five, not two:** `types.rs:218-219`, `derived/request.rs:48,49,233`, plus `request/tests.rs:104` size assertion. The compile-only harness also hardcodes `FAMILIES` and textual arms (`tests/derived_view_capabilities.rs:48-61,189-201`) and must be generated from `ALL` or it becomes a second inventory.

10. **Injection sites the design must name.** Owner constructions at `relations.rs:78,93`, lazily created external owner at `workspace.rs:132,139`, and `pipeline/service.rs:824`. `operators` is still absent from `crate-layers` (`crates/ryo/tastemaker.toml:19`) and `internal-packages` already lists a nonexistent `idiomatic-sh-ryo-derived` (`tastemaker.toml:8`); the TM rule at line 35 is intra-crate only.
