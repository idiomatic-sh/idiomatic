# public-fields 0.3.0

A field must be at least as visible as its struct, where 0.2.0 asked every field to be `pub`: a `pub(crate)` struct with `pub(crate)` fields, an engine's record sealed to its crate, passes, and making its fields `pub` would have undone the seal. A field narrower than its struct is still reported: a private or `pub(crate)` field of a `pub` struct, a private or `pub(super)` field of a `pub(crate)` struct. Scenarios `crate-sealed-type-with-crate-fields` (passes) and `crate-sealed-type-with-a-narrower-field` (reported); the 0.2.0 scenarios hold unchanged. Everything else, the `compile_fail` proof among it, is as in 0.2.0.
